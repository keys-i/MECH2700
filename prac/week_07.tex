\section*{Question 1.}

Write the aircraft equation of motion as a system of two first-order ODEs.

\begin{solution}
  The equation of motion is
  \[
    m\ddot{x} = F - \frac{1}{2}C_D A_{\mathrm{ref}}\rho V^2
  \]

  Define the velocity as
  \[
    V = \dot{x}
  \]

  Therefore,
  \[
    \dot{V} = \ddot{x}
  \]

  Dividing the equation of motion by the mass gives
  \[
    \dot{V} = \frac{F}{m} - \frac{C_D A_{\mathrm{ref}}\rho}{2m}V^2
  \]

  Hence, the required first-order system is
  \[
    \boxed{
        \begin{aligned}
            \frac{dx}{dt} &= V \\[0.5em]
            \frac{dV}{dt} &= \frac{F}{m}
            - \frac{C_D A_{\mathrm{ref}}\rho}{2m}
            V^2
        \end{aligned}
    }
  \]

  Using
  \[
    \begin{aligned}
      F    & = 242000\ \mathrm{N},        & m                & = 70000\ \mathrm{kg} \\
      C_D  & = 0.08,                      & A_{\mathrm{ref}} & = 125\ \mathrm{m^2}  \\
      \rho & = 1.225\ \mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}
    \end{aligned}
  \]

  gives
  \[
    \frac{F}{m} = \frac{242000}{70000} \approx 3.457143\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}
  \]

  and
  \[
    \frac{C_D A_{\mathrm{ref}}\rho}{2m} = \frac{(0.08)(125)(1.225)}{2(70000)}
                                        = 8.75 \times 10^{-5}\ \mathrm{m^{-1}}
  \]

  With position and velocity expressed in SI units,
  \[
    \boxed{
        \begin{aligned}
          \frac{dx}{dt} &= V \\[0.5em]
          \frac{dV}{dt} &= \frac{242000 -
          6.125V^2}{70000}
        \end{aligned}
    }
  \]

  Taking the state vector as
  \[
    \mathbf{y} = \begin{bmatrix}
                   x \\
                   v
                 \end{bmatrix}
  \]

  the system can be written as
  \[
    \boxed{
        \frac{d\mathbf{y}}{dt}
            = \mathbf{f}(t,\mathbf{y})
            = \begin{bmatrix}
                V \\[0.5em]
                \dfrac{242000 - 6.125V^2}{70000}
            \end{bmatrix}
    }
  \]
\end{solution}
